# 0006. The path from an intent to what was delivered can be traced and scored

For any intent, dodona can show each link from the plain words to the running system, how strong each link is, and every exception that was granted on the way.

## Criteria

| # | Criterion | Gate | Checked by |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Intent status, gates, assurance and amendments are modelled, with a planted defect for each rule | hard | Not yet covered (claims C23 to C27) |
| 2 | A self-granted exception is always shown first and never counts where an independent approver is required | hard | Not yet covered (claim C25) |
| 3 | The intent and amendment formats have schemas | hard | Not yet covered |
