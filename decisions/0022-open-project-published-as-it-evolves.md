# 0022. An open project, published as it evolves

## Context

dodona should be useful to everyone and should gain collaborators, more use cases and more stability from them. A design shown only when finished gets no early challenge.

## Decision

The repository is public and the design is published as it changes, with drafts clearly marked as drafts. Contributions are welcome from people and from agents, under the same rules: a change of direction is a proposed decision record, a claim comes with the check that could show it false, and nothing is described as verified unless it was.

Discussion and proposals happen in the open on the project's issue tracker.

## Consequences

- Readers will see unfinished and sometimes wrong material; each document states its status.
- Being usable by a newcomer becomes a requirement: a contributor who cannot follow the documents is a defect in the documents.

## Alternatives rejected

- Publish when ready: fewer rough edges in public, and no outside view while the design is cheapest to change.

## What would show this wrong

A newcomer cannot tell from the repository what is decided, what is proposed and what has actually been checked.
